## Entry Points for Contributors

**Start anywhere. Fork freely. No permission needed.**

### Story Entry Points

- Write a scene where a minor character (a fuller, a scribe, a grain merchant) attempts to explain their need for complexity, ornament, or 'waste' to Lucius, and he dissects their argument with ruthless minimalism.
- Create a monologue from Decima's perspective, reflecting on serving three generations of Valerii men, each stripping away more from the house and their lives, and what she has hidden away in the kitchen that she considers essential.
- Write the encounter where Gallio, the man from the Quirinal, finally corners Lucius. Does he try to bribe, threaten, or philosophize? How does Lucius's minimalist logic become a weapon?
- Imagine a chapter from the perspective of the single cypress tree in the garden. What has it witnessed? What does it need that the stark, human world of the house does not provide?

### Technical Entry Points

- Build a 'Minimalist Logic Engine'—a text-based tool where users input a complex problem (e.g., 'distribute grain to a starving district') and the system, following Lucius's principles, strips away all moral, political, and social considerations to output the most efficient, bare-path solution. Visualize the discarded elements as wood shavings.
- Create an interactive map of Lucius's Rome that only displays the routes he uses (the *angiporti*, the warehouse path). All other streets, forums, and temples are blank. As users click on the few visible locations, they get only the minimal, functional data Lucius would care about (price, weight, route security).
